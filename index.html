What is the Chicagoland Cup?

The Chicagoland Cup is a 5-Event Series held one Monday a month as a subset of our regular season! Events count toward Local APT Chicago Player of the Year points, National APT Player of the Year points, AND the Chicagoland Cup.

Your Top 3 point finishes in these 5 events count toward the Cup! Division Winners receive a trophy and prize money at each event, and one Champion in each Division with the most points accumulated in their Top 3 finishes will win the Cup!
